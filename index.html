    Calls it handles every day

    Same receptionist, four very different calls.

    Reschedule
    "I can't make my cleaning Friday. Is there anything next week?"

    Front desk gets: the existing visit, two compatible openings the patient accepted, and which one they prefer.

    Returning caller
    "Hi, I called on Monday about whitening."

    It remembers: the earlier request went to the office on Monday. It says so plainly, adds today's details to the same request and never promises what staff haven't confirmed.

    Urgent
    "My crown came off and it really hurts."

    It escalates: asks about pain and swelling, texts the on-call phone right away and tells the caller exactly what happens next.

    Questions
    "Do you take my insurance, and are you open Saturdays?"

    It answers from your own office details, including holiday closures you post, then offers to set up a visit.
